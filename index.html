Born out of passion in Malaysia, Shoyu Bros is small-batch artisanal soy sauce crafted slowly, the way legendary shoyu is meant to be. Made with non-GMO Canadian soybeans, roasted wheat, and sea salt brine, it spends 9 full months basking under the equatorial sun in earthenware jars before being pressed once and infused with aromatic wild garlic.

Instead of aggressive industrial saltiness, Shoyu Bros reveals deep, layered umami with an enchanting earthy, mushroom-like fragrance that some compare to black truffles.

Taste what shoyu was always meant to be.

MADE THE SHOYU BROS WAY

Naturally fermented using Aspergillus sojae mold, aged 9 months in earthen clay urns, then slow-pressed once through fine cloth to preserve the natural fragrance and oils.

4 INGREDIENTS · ZERO ADDED MSG

THE BROTHERS BEHIND THE BREW 兄弟の情熱

MEET
THE BROS

"Great things can happen too in a pandemic," featured the Malay Mail in June 2021. Shoyu Bros began with a simple craving for honest, full-bodied soy sauce that honored ancestral craft.

Frustrated by watered-down commercial sauces loaded with preservatives and artificial flavor enhancers, we began fermenting non-GMO soybeans in earthenware jars right here in Southeast Asia.

When we infused our first press with fresh wild garlic, the kitchen filled with an aroma so earthy and magnetic that some compared it to truffles. Food critics at Eat Drink KL were quick to discover its magic — writing about our garlic shoyu starring in everything from Spanish paella to Mexican tacos and local wantan mee.

Every single bottle is still small-batch blended and sealed with the exact same passion that started it all.
